Top recommendation

Start with 2, then 1

Candidate 2 (JevClient, one head) is the smaller change and removes the getattr discovery that candidates 1 and 5 would otherwise build on. Candidate 1 (answer cache) then gets one telemetry seam to own, and it is the one that stops the ticket-10 bug class from coming back.
